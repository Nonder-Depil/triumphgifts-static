Custom Medals

Celebrate achievements with custom medals that reflect true honor and prestige. We manufacture a wide variety of medals including sports medals, marathon medals, academic medals, corporate awards, commemorative medals, and recognition medals. Using zinc alloy, brass, or iron, we can produce 2D, 3D, hollow-out, or multi-layer designs with soft enamel, hard enamel, antique plating, or sandblasted finishes.

Our factory-direct customization services allow you to choose custom ribbon widths, textures, and printing, as well as engraved names, event dates, or serial numbers. We also provide special effects like glitter, translucent enamel, dual plating, and custom shapes to make your medals unique.

Perfect for school competitions, sports events, employee recognition, military honors, or charity fundraisers, our medals come with professional design support, fast sample proofing, and affordable wholesale pricing. Whether you need exclusive limited-edition medals or large quantities for events, we ensure precision craftsmanship and durable quality.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Need a specific finish or unusual shape? Send the artwork and intended use for a production recommendation.

What materials are available for custom medals?

Pretty Shiny Gifts offers various materials for custom medals, including zinc alloy, iron, brass, aluminum, and stainless steel. We recommend the best material based on your design, budget, and application.

Can I customize the shape and size of my medals?

Yes. We can create custom medals in any shape and size, including logo shapes, sports designs, 3D medals, and unique creative structures based on your ideas.

What finishing options are available for custom medals?

We provide multiple finishes, including gold, silver, copper, black nickel, antique, matte, and two-tone plating to achieve different styles and effects.

Can I add colors or logos to my medals?

Yes. We offer soft enamel, hard enamel, transparent enamel, glitter, glow-in-the-dark, and UV printing options to make your medals more personalized.

Do you offer 2D and 3D custom medals?

Yes. We manufacture both 2D and 3D medals with detailed designs, raised elements, and premium finishes for sports events, awards, and commemorative projects.

Can you help with my medal design?

Yes. Our design team can help turn your logo, sketch, or idea into a production-ready medal design and provide artwork confirmation before production.

Are medal ribbons customizable?

Yes. Medal ribbons can be customized with colors, logos, patterns, and printing options to match your event or brand requirements.

Can I customize the back side of the medal?

Yes. The back of the medal can include logos, event details, dates, rankings, serial numbers, or personalized engraving.

What packaging options are available?

We provide different packaging options, including poly bags, velvet pouches, plastic boxes, velvet boxes, and custom gift packaging.

Why choose Pretty Shiny Gifts for custom medals?

With over 40 years of manufacturing experience, Pretty Shiny Gifts specializes in custom medals with professional design support, advanced production, strict quality control, and reliable factory service.
