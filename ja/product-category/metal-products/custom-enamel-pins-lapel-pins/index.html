Custom Lapel Pins & Enamel Pins

Our custom enamel pins combine premium metal raw materials, mature die-striking/die casting and epoxy coating crafts to deliver long-lasting decorative lapel pins for all custom scenarios. We source high-grade zinc alloy, iron, brass and imported non-fading enamel glaze as base materials, with multi-layer electroplating treatment to resist rust, oxidation and color fading.

Different from general promotional gifts factories, we tailor two core series for your selection: genuine hard enamel pins (Champleve pins) with smooth jewelry-grade surfaces and soft enamel pins with vivid layered color textures. All custom enamel pins pass full pre-shipment quality inspection, holding global safety certifications to meet EU, US and Australia import standards.

Our main client groups cover brand enterprises, charity organizations, sports event organizers, military uniform suppliers and souvenir retail merchants. No matter you need branded uniform decorations, campaign giveaway gifts or collectible badge sets, our custom lapel pins can fully match your personalized design demands.

Product Types

Lapel Pins Types & Unique Custom Enamel Pins Features

True Hard Enamel Lapel Pins (Real Cloisonne)

Premium genuine enamel crafted with high-temperature firing and manual fine polishing. Boasts a fully flat, mirror-smooth, scratch-resistant and fade-proof surface with high-end texture and long service life. Supports dual-tone plating, delicate 3D sculpting and glow-in-the-dark effects. Perfect for high-end corporate gifts, military badges, car emblems and collection-grade custom badges.

Imitation Hard Enamel Lapel Pins

Premium imitation hard enamel features a nearly flat polished surface, bridging the texture gap between genuine hard enamel and soft enamel. With fine color filling, smooth finish and excellent wear resistance, it delivers a high-end look at a more budget-friendly cost. It supports dual-tone plating, fine line details and various custom special effects. Ideal for mid-to-high-end corporate gifts, brand promotional badges and bulk customized premium souvenirs.

Soft Enamel Lapel Pins

Classic soft enamel craft with raised metal outlines and sunken colored filling, creating a distinct tactile concave-convex texture. It offers ultra-rich color palettes, glitter, pearlescent and gradient color customizations with highly flexible design compatibility. Featuring super-fast sampling, short production cycle and ultra-low bulk cost, it is the most popular option for large-volume orders. Perfect for event souvenirs, fan merch, club badges, casual promotional giveaways and daily custom pins.

3D Custom Lapel Pins

Made via integral stereo embossing and deep relief carving, delivering outstanding 3D layering and tactile texture. Compatible with hard enamel, soft enamel and vintage plating processes. Suitable for military commemorative badges, limited-edition collectibles, honor medals and high-end memorial custom crafts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Need a specific finish or unusual shape? Send the artwork and intended use for a production recommendation.

What’s the MOQ for wholesale custom enamel badges?

Our MOQ for mass production custom enamel pins is 50 pieces; we also accept single custom sample orders for design verification.

Do you offer free design for custom lapel pins bulk orders?

Yes, our professional design team provides free drawing modification and layout design based on your pin order ideas or pictures.

How fast is lead time for bulk custom enamel badges?

Samples take 3-10 working days; mass production finishes within 12-25 working days after sample confirmation, plus shipping transit time.

Do you offer refund or rework if the custom enamel pins have quality defects?

Absolutely. We will re-produce new pins or provide full refunds for any defective products caused by our production errors.

Can you ship custom lapel pins worldwide?

Yes. We ship to customers worldwide via DHL, FedEx, UPS, air freight, and sea freight, providing the most suitable shipping solution based on your order quantity and delivery schedule.

Can you help if I don't have a finished design?

Yes. Our experienced design team offers free artwork and design assistance. Just send us your logo, sketch, or concept, and we'll prepare a professional digital proof for your approval.

What file formats do you accept for artwork?

We accept AI, EPS, PDF, PSD, CDR, SVG, JPG, PNG, and even hand sketches. If your artwork needs adjustment, our designers will optimize it free of charge before production.

Can you match Pantone colors?

Yes. We use the Pantone Matching System (PMS) whenever possible to ensure your custom lapel pins accurately match your brand or design colors.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept T/T (bank transfer), PayPal, and other international payment methods. Our sales team will recommend the most suitable option based on your order.

Why choose Triumph Gifts as your custom lapel pin manufacturer?

With over 40+ years of manufacturing experience, factory-direct pricing, free artwork, strict quality control, fast production, and worldwide shipping, Triumph Gifts has become a trusted OEM partner for brands, organizations, wholesalers, and distributors around the world.

Can you produce lapel pins based on my logo?

Yes. Simply send us your logo, artwork, or even a sketch, and our designers will create a FREE digital proof for your approval before production. We support OEM & ODM custom lapel pin manufacturing in virtually any shape, size, color, or finish to match your branding requirements.

Is there a mold fee for custom lapel pins?

Most custom lapel pin projects require a one-time mold fee based on the design and size. For repeat orders using the same mold within 3 years, no additional mold fee is required.

Are your lapel pins compliant with international standards?

Yes. Our products can comply with international quality and safety standards, including EN71 & CPSIA and other customer-required testing. Raw Material certification documents are available upon request.
