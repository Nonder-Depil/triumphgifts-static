Custom Chenille Patches Manufacturer | Retro Varsity Badges Factory

We are a premier China OEM/ODM manufacturer specializing in custom chenille patches, supplying global sportswear brands, school varsity suppliers, boutique fashion labels, and event organizers.

Chenille patches are celebrated for their distinctive, fuzzy, vaulted yarn loops combined with crisp embroidered borders or felt backing. Often seen on traditional American letterman jackets, retro baseball apparel, and modern luxury streetwear, chenille delivers an unmistakable 3D tactile feel and nostalgic visual charm that flat stitching simply cannot match.

Operating our own 64,000 m² factory equipped with computerized multi-needle chenille machines, we handle everything from classic varsity letters to complex multi-layered mascot designs with precision. All products meet US CPSIA standards and European EN71 safety regulations, supported by international audits including SEDEX 4P and ISO9001 certification.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Need a specific finish or unusual shape? Send the artwork and intended use for a production recommendation.

What makes chenille patches different from embroidered or woven patches?

Chenille patches use yarn loops that create a soft, plush, carpet-like 3D feel, making them famously retro and tactile. Embroidered and woven patches use flat, thin thread stitching. Chenille is ideal for large, bold designs like letterman letters, while embroidery/woven is better suited for tiny, detailed text.

Can complex, small details be made with chenille yarn?

Because chenille yarn is thick and fuzzy, very small details or text under 8mm cannot be looped clearly. For complex logos, we produce Chenille + Embroidery Hybrid Patches, using soft chenille for the main body and fine embroidery thread for small details and lettering.

What is the best way to attach chenille patches to jackets?

While iron-on backing is available, sewing is strongly recommended for chenille patches. Because chenille patches are thicker and heavier than standard patches, stitching along the felt border ensures long-lasting durability on heavy jackets, wool coats, and hoodies.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom chenille patches?

Our standard MOQ is 50 pieces per design. Contact our sales team to discuss multi-letter or custom color-way options for bulk wholesale orders.

Are your chenille materials safe for apparel export?

Yes! All yarn, felt bases, and thread dyes are non-toxic, eco-friendly, and fully compliant with US CPSIA and European EN71 safety regulations, making them safe for kids, youth merchandise, and international brand retail.
