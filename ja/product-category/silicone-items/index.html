シリコーン・アイテム

Our factory specializes in custom silicone products, known for their flexibility, durability, and eco-friendly properties. We produce a wide selection of silicone wristbands, keychains, drinkware sleeves, phone holders, luggage tags, and kitchenware items, all fully customizable with debossed, embossed, printed, or color-filled logos.

Perfect for events, charity fundraising, sports teams, schools, and promotional campaigns, our silicone items are non-toxic, waterproof, and long-lasting. We offer OEM solutions, wholesale pricing, and fast production turnaround to meet different branding needs.

With endless customization options—colors, patterns, glow-in-the-dark effects, or multi-layer designs—you can create unique silicone gifts that perfectly represent your brand.
