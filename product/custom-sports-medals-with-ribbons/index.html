    Custom Sports Medals with Ribbons – Fully Customizable Options

    At Triumph Gifts, we offer fully customizable custom sports medals with ribbons, allowing you to choose every detail—from colors, sizes, designs, shapes, materials to logos. With over 20 years of industry expertise, we are committed to delivering high-quality, personalized medals that add prestige and distinction to your sports events.

    Beyond traditional round medals, we provide custom shapes tailored to your event’s unique theme. Whether you prefer distinctive geometric designs or shapes that highlight your logo, we craft standout sports medals that truly represent your brand and occasion.

    Available Ribbons for Custom Sports Medals with Ribbons

    We offer a wide selection of ribbon options to perfectly complement your Custom Sports Medals with Ribbons . There is no setup charge for stock colors, making it easy and cost-effective to get started. If you’re looking for something unique, custom patterns, lengths, and sizes are fully supported to meet your specific branding or event theme.

    In addition to standard neck ribbons, we also provide short ribbons, pin attachments, and clutch back options to accommodate different display or wearing preferences. Whether you’re preparing medals for sports events, corporate recognition, or school awards, our ribbon solutions are designed to enhance both function and appearance.

    Custom Sports Medals with Ribbons – Plating Color Options

    Custom Sports Medals with Ribbons are available in a variety of high-quality plating color options, including Silver, Gold, Copper, Antique Silver, Antique Gold, and Antique Copper. These finishes offer a wide range of visual effects—from modern elegance to vintage charm—ensuring your medals reflect the tone and prestige of your event. Whether you’re looking for a luxurious shine or a timeless antique look, our plating options transform standard medals into exceptional keepsakes.
