    Custom Motel Keychains

    Retro Plastic Key Tags for Hotels & Brands

    Bring back the charm of vintage roadside stays with our custom plastic motel keychains. Lightweight, colorful, and fully printable—perfect for hotels, fashion brands, events, or nostalgic gifts.

    Inquire Now

    100% Custom Made PVC Rubber Keychains

    Inspired by the golden age of American travel, our custom motel keychains offer a timeless retro look with modern customization. Made from durable injection-molded ABS or acrylic plastic, each keychain features the iconic diamond/oval shape seen in classic motels and diners.

    These keychains are lightweight, smooth, and ideal for full-color printing, engraving, or foil stamping. Whether you’re a boutique hotel, a lifestyle brand, a band promoting new merch, or simply a fan of vintage vibes, these keychains offer a stylish, low-cost way to make a big impression.

    Customize your design with:
    – Hotel-style room numbers
    – Logos, slogans, or witty quotes
    – QR codes or event details
    – Glitter, transparent colors, or matte finishes

    Popular among fashion retailers, online shops, and event planners, these keychains serve equally well as functional key tags or collectible accessories.

    Specifications

    Material: Durable plastic (ABS, acrylic, or recycled plastic available)
    Classic motel-style shape (diamond/oval)
    Custom printing: screen print, UV print, engraving, or foil stamp
    Full range of retro & modern colors (glitter, neon, translucent)
    Lightweight and affordable for mass distribution
    Great for hotel branding, creative merch, or promotional gifts
    Attachment: standard metal keyring or ball chain
