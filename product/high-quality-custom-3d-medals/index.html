    Common Questions About OEM 3D Custom Medals

    1. What customization options are available for high-quality 3D custom medals?

    We offer a wide range of customization options including materials such as zinc alloy, brass, iron, or stainless steel, plating colors, sizes, shapes, designs, and logo integration. You can select specific finishes, add personalized engravings, and choose unique shapes to perfectly match your event’s theme.

    2. What is the minimum order quantity for high-quality 3D custom medals?

    We have no minimum order quantity. Simply send us your design to start your order process.

    3. How long does it take to produce high-quality 3D custom medals?

    Production time varies depending on design complexity and order size. Typically, it takes approximately 3-5 weeks from design approval to delivery. Expedited services are available for urgent orders to ensure timely arrival for your event.

    4. Can I see a sample before placing a large order for high-quality 3D custom medals?

    Yes, we provide sample medals for approval before you commit to a full order. Sample production usually takes 3-7 days, allowing you to evaluate the design, materials, and overall quality to ensure it meets your expectations.

    5. What materials are used for high-quality 3D custom medals?

    Our high-quality 3D custom medals are crafted from durable and premium materials including zinc alloy, brass, iron, and stainless steel, chosen for their longevity and superior finish.
