Product Types

Main Types of Custom Woven Patches

As an experienced custom patch manufacturer, we specialize in crafting personalized woven patches for apparel brands, uniforms, tactical gear, corporate promotional events, and sports clubs. Choose from various weaving densities, border finishes, and special threads to create your perfect patch.

Classic High-Density Woven Patches

Thin thread structure allows ultra-fine detail, sharp typography, and intricate graphic reproduction. Flat, smooth surface with long-lasting durability for daily wear.

Laser Cut Custom Shaped Woven Patches

Precision laser cutting allows complex geometric contours and unique irregular die-cut shapes without fraying, perfect for modern brand logos and streetwear.

Merrowed Border Woven Patches

Features a classic, thick wrapped thread edge that gives the patch a traditional, sturdy 3D frame while preserving high-definition central graphics.

Metallic & Special Thread Woven Patches

Incorporates shiny metallic gold/silver thread, neon colors, or glow-in-the-dark threads into the design for elevated visual impact and premium merchandise value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Need a specific finish or unusual shape? Send the artwork and intended use for a production recommendation.

What is the main difference between woven patches and embroidered patches?

Woven patches use much finer threads woven closely together, resulting in a smooth, flat surface that can capture extremely fine detail and small text (down to 2mm). Embroidered patches use thicker threads stitched onto a base fabric, creating a raised, textured 3D look ideal for bolder graphics.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom woven patches?

Our standard MOQ is 50 pieces per design. For trial orders or multi-design projects, please contact our sales team for flexible wholesale solutions.

Are iron-on woven patches washable and long-lasting?

Yes, our iron-on heat seal backing uses commercial-grade adhesive designed to withstand regular machine washing. For items subjected to extreme wear or industrial laundering, we recommend adding a quick perimeter stitch for permanent hold.

Can you match exact Pantone colors for our brand logo?

Absolutely. We use the Pantone Solid Coated color matching system to source exact thread shades, ensuring 100% brand color consistency across all bulk production runs.

Are your woven patches safe for export to the US and European markets?

Yes. All our woven threads and backing adhesives are eco-friendly, lead-free, and comply with CPSIA (USA) and EN71 (EU) international safety regulations. Material test reports are available upon request.
