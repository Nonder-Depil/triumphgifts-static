Quality Assurance Blueprint

Standardized 8-Stage Manufacturing Flow

Every production batch progresses through calibrated metallurgical and finishing checkpoints to guarantee structural integrity and surface uniformity.

Phase 01

CAD Engineering Assessment

Vector line weight verification, 3D relief depth simulation, minimal wall thickness check, and mold release draft planning.

Phase 02

CNC & EDM Tooling

Sub-micron steel die milling on high-speed CNC machining centers, followed by precision spark erosion for razor-sharp contours.

Phase 03

High-Pressure Die-Casting

Automated hot-chamber zinc alloy casting eliminating internal cavities and yielding uniform metallurgical grain density.

Phase 04

Precision Color Dispensing

Pantone PMS color-matched liquid resin application via pneumatic dispensers and artisan hand-filling for intricate recesses.

Phase 05

Controlled Thermal Curing

Multi-zone convection ovens bake colors at calibrated temperatures to achieve scratch-resistant, UV-resilient enamel polymerization.

Phase 06

Automated Electroplating

Computerized plating sequence for genuine 24K gold, silver, nickel, antique bronze, and black nickel with uniform micron coverage.

Phase 07

Mechanical Buffing & Assembly

Progressive felt wheel polishing removes microscopic parting lines before ultrasonic degreasing and hardware silver-soldering.

Phase 08

100% Optical QC & Packing

Every piece inspected under high-CRI lighting for bubbles, plating voids, and pinback tension prior to barcoded retail boxing.
