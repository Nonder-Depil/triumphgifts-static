UV Printed Custom Medals: High-Quality Personalized Awards for Every Occasion

  • Triumph Gifts
  • 2025-06-05

At Triumph Gifts, we take pride in offering top-tier UV printed custom medals designed to meet the needs of a wide range of events — from competitive sports and school tournaments to corporate giveaways and special commemorations. Leveraging advanced UV printing technology, our custom medals combine vibrant color reproduction with durable materials to produce stunning awards that leave a lasting impression.

Why UV Printing is Ideal for Custom Medals

Unlike traditional enamel-filled medals, UV printing allows full-color designs — including gradients, complex patterns, and photographic images — to be printed directly onto the medal’s surface. This process not only enhances visual appeal but also offers several key benefits:

  • True-to-Design Color Reproduction: UV printing accurately captures your original artwork without simplifying or altering the design, maintaining fine details and subtle color transitions.

  • Printing on 3D and Raised Surfaces: The UV ink adheres well to uneven or raised medal features, enabling colorful customization on textured areas that are challenging for conventional methods.

  • Fast Production Turnaround: Since UV printing does not require molds for colors, you can get multiple color variants on the same medal mold quickly and cost-effectively.

  • Eco-Friendly Inks: We use environmentally friendly eco-solvent UV inks that minimize harmful emissions and support sustainable manufacturing.

  • Versatility in Finishes: Choose from glossy or matte finishes to match the aesthetic of your brand or event, with medals made from sturdy zinc alloy for lasting quality.

Specifications of Our Custom UV Printed Medals

  • Material: High-quality Zinc Alloy

  • Design: One-sided 3D mold with intricate details

  • Size: 2.5 inches diameter (excluding loop), 3mm thickness

  • Finish Options: Shiny polished or matte finish

  • Printing Method: Advanced ultraviolet (UV) printing technology

  • Attachment: Customized heat transfer printed ribbon (900x25mm), available in your choice of colors and designs

Applications of Custom UV Printed Medals

  • Sports Events: From marathons to cycling races, our medals are designed to celebrate athletes’ achievements with bright, durable finishes.

  • Corporate Promotions: Custom medals make memorable giveaways and employee recognition awards that enhance brand visibility.

  • School & University Awards: Perfect for academic achievements, competitions, and graduation ceremonies.

  • Community & Charity Events: A great way to recognize participants and sponsors while promoting your cause with colorful, lasting keepsakes.
