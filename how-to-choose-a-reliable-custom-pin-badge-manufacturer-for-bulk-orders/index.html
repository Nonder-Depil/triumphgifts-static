How to Choose a Reliable Custom Pin Badge Manufacturer for Bulk Orders

  • Triumph Gifts
  • 2026-01-06

When sourcing custom pin badges for branding, promotions, or retail, one of the most common questions buyers ask manufacturers is whether to choose soft enamel pin badges or hard enamel pin badges.
Although both are produced using similar base processes, the differences in manufacturing, finish, durability, and cost can significantly affect the final result.

This guide explains the key differences from a pin badge manufacturer’s perspective, helping B2B buyers make informed decisions for bulk and OEM orders.

What Are Soft Enamel Pin Badges?

Soft enamel pin badges are produced by stamping or casting metal and filling recessed areas with colored enamel paint. After coloring, the enamel sits slightly below the raised metal lines, creating a textured surface that highlights design details.

Our Customization Capabilities

  • Metal stamping or zinc alloy casting

  • Enamel colors filled below metal outlines

  • Optional epoxy coating for surface protection

Soft enamel pin badges are widely used for promotional campaigns and event giveaways due to their cost efficiency and design flexibility.

What Are Hard Enamel Pin Badges?

Hard enamel pin badges use a similar base process but involve multiple rounds of enamel filling and high-temperature polishing. The enamel is polished flush with the metal surface, resulting in a smooth, glass-like finish.

Manufacturing Characteristics

  • Multiple enamel filling and baking stages

  • Surface polishing for a flat finish

  • Higher production time and cost

Hard enamel pin badges are often chosen for premium branding, corporate recognition, and retail merchandise.

Key Manufacturing Differences Between Soft and Hard Enamel Pin Badges

1. Surface Finish

Soft enamel pin badges have a textured surface with raised metal lines, while hard enamel pin badges feature a smooth, flat finish where metal and enamel are level.

2. Production Process Complexity

Hard enamel pin badges require more manufacturing steps, including repeated baking and polishing. Soft enamel pin badges follow a more streamlined process, making them faster to produce in bulk.

3. Durability and Wear Resistance

Hard enamel pin badges are generally more scratch-resistant due to their polished surface. Soft enamel pin badges remain durable but may require epoxy coating for enhanced protection.

4. Cost and Bulk Pricing

From a factory perspective:

  • Soft enamel pin badges are more cost-effective for large quantities

  • Hard enamel pin badges involve higher labor and production costs

For bulk orders, soft enamel options often provide better cost control.

5. Design Suitability

  • Soft enamel pin badges are ideal for designs with fine lines, gradients, or complex artwork

  • Hard enamel pin badges work best for clean, bold designs and corporate logos

A professional custom pin badge manufacturer will typically review artwork and recommend the most suitable option.

Which Option Is Better for Bulk Custom Pin Badges?

The choice depends on your project goals:

  • Soft enamel pin badges are suitable for promotional use, campaigns, and large-volume orders

  • Hard enamel pin badges are preferred for premium branding and long-term use

For OEM buyers and distributors, balancing design requirements, budget, and lead time is key when selecting enamel types.

Manufacturer Recommendation

Experienced pin badge factories often suggest starting with soft enamel pin badges for first-time bulk orders due to their flexibility and shorter production cycles. Hard enamel pin badges are recommended when brand positioning requires a refined, high-end appearance.

If you are sourcing directly from a custom pin badges factory, consulting the manufacturer early in the design stage helps avoid unnecessary revisions and cost increases.

FAQ

What are custom pin badges?

Custom pin badges are personalized metal badges manufactured based on a client’s design, logo, or artwork. They are commonly used for corporate branding, promotional campaigns, events, clubs, and retail merchandise. Custom pin badges are produced in bulk by specialized manufacturers using stamping or casting processes.

How are custom pin badges manufactured?

Custom pin badges are manufactured through a multi-step process including mold creation, metal stamping or casting, electroplating, enamel coloring, polishing, and final inspection. Professional pin badge manufacturers handle most processes in-house to ensure quality consistency and accurate color matching.

What materials are used for custom pin badges?

Custom pin badges are typically made from iron, zinc alloy, brass, or stainless steel. Material selection depends on design complexity, budget, and usage requirements. Zinc alloy is commonly used for detailed designs, while iron and brass are preferred for classic enamel pin badges.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom pin badges?

The MOQ for custom pin badges varies by manufacturer and design, but most factories support bulk production starting from a few hundred pieces. Lower MOQs may be available for repeat orders or simplified designs, especially when working directly with a pin badge factory.

How long does custom pin badge production take?

Production time for custom pin badges usually includes sampling and mass production. Samples typically take 5–7 working days, while bulk production requires 10–20 working days depending on order quantity, finishing options, and production capacity.

What customization options are available for custom pin badges?

Custom pin badges can be fully customized in shape, size, material, plating color, enamel type, and backing options. Manufacturers also support OEM services such as custom packaging, backing cards, and private label branding for bulk buyers.

Are custom pin badges suitable for corporate and OEM orders?

Yes. Custom pin badges are widely used for corporate branding, employee recognition, promotional giveaways, and OEM resale projects. Manufacturers with export experience can support large-volume orders, private labeling, and international shipping requirements.

Conclusion

Understanding the manufacturing differences between soft enamel and hard enamel pin badges allows buyers to align design expectations with production realities. Both options offer reliable performance when produced by an experienced manufacturer, but each serves different branding and budget needs.

For bulk orders, working directly with a professional custom pin badge manufacturer ensures accurate material selection, stable quality control, and efficient production timelines.
