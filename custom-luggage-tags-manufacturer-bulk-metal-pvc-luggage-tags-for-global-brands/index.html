Introduction

Custom luggage tags are widely used in the global travel and promotional product industry due to their practical function and long-term branding exposure. Every suitcase traveling through airports, hotels, and business trips becomes a mobile advertising platform.

For B2B buyers such as airlines, travel agencies, corporate brands, and promotional distributors, custom luggage tags are not just accessories—they are scalable branding tools with measurable marketing value.

As a professional manufacturer, Triumph Gifts produces high-quality custom luggage tags in bulk, including custom metal luggage tags and custom PVC luggage tags, supporting OEM/ODM projects worldwide.

Why Custom Luggage Tags Are High-ROI Promotional Products

Unlike disposable marketing materials, luggage tags stay with users for years. This creates continuous brand exposure at no additional cost.

Key Business Benefits

  • Long-term brand visibility
  • High usage frequency in travel environments
  • Strong practicality increases retention rate
  • Suitable for global distribution campaigns
  • Low cost per impression (CPI)
  • Ideal for corporate gifting and travel branding

Custom Metal vs PVC Luggage Tags Comparison

Pricing depends on multiple manufacturing and design factors:

1. Material Selection

Metal is more expensive due to tooling and finishing processes.

2. Order Quantity

Higher volume significantly reduces unit cost.

3. Design Complexity

  • 3D structures increase mold cost
  • Multiple colors increase production time
  • Special finishes require additional processing

4. Packaging Requirements

Custom packaging such as gift boxes increases overall cost.

5. Personalization

Adding names, QR codes, or serial numbers affects production cost.
