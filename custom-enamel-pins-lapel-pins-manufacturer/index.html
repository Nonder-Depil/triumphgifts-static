Custom Hard Enamel Pins

Custom hard enamel pins are polished flat until the enamel and metal surfaces become level, creating a smooth jewelry-grade finish with excellent durability and long-lasting appearance.

Hard enamel pins are widely used for:

  • Corporate recognition
  • Executive branding
  • Employee awards
  • Government badges
  • Luxury retail merchandise
  • Commemorative collections
  • Premium souvenir products

Advantages of hard enamel pins:

  • Smooth polished surface
  • High perceived product value
  • Excellent scratch resistance
  • Durable long-term finish
  • Professional appearance

Custom Soft Enamel Pins

Custom soft enamel pins feature raised metal outlines with recessed enamel colors, creating textured surfaces and stronger visual depth. Soft enamel remains one of the most popular manufacturing options for promotional products and collectible merchandise.

Soft enamel pins are ideal for:

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between hard enamel pins and soft enamel pins?

Hard enamel pins have a smooth polished surface with higher durability, while soft enamel pins feature raised metal outlines with recessed enamel colors for stronger texture and vibrant appearance.

What materials are used for custom enamel pins?

Common materials include zinc alloy, iron, brass, copper, and stainless steel depending on design requirements and production budgets.

Can you produce custom-shaped enamel pins?

Yes. We support fully customized shapes, layered structures, hollow cutouts, and 3D sculpted lapel pin designs.

What plating options are available?

Available finishes include gold plating, nickel plating, black nickel, antique finishes, rainbow plating, matte plating, and dual-tone plating.

Do you provide OEM & ODM enamel pin manufacturing?

Yes. We provide complete OEM & ODM production services including mold development, sampling, custom packaging, and large-scale manufacturing.

Can you handle bulk wholesale enamel pin orders?

Yes. Our factory supports wholesale enamel pin production for distributors, promotional companies, fashion brands, and global retail markets.

Do you provide worldwide shipping?

Yes. We export custom enamel pins and lapel pins worldwide with professional international shipping support.
