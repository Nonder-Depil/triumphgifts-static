Custom Sequin Patches Manufacturer | Reversible Sequin Badges Factory

We are a leading China OEM/ODM manufacturer specializing in custom sequin patches, supplying international fashion labels, kids’ apparel brands, dancewear designers, and boutique event organizers.

Sequin patches (also known as spangle or paillette badges) are crafted by stitching hundreds of overlapping reflective metallic disc sequins onto a durable felt or mesh base. Whether utilizing classic single-color glittering sequins or innovative two-tone flip/reversible sequins that change artwork with a simple swipe of the hand, sequin patches offer an interactive, high-end visual effect that captures immediate attention under light.

Operating our own 64,000 m² factory equipped with computerized automatic sequin embroidery machinery, we ensure tight sequin attachment, snag-free edges, and vibrant color retention. All materials meet US CPSIA standards and EU EN71 safety regulations, supported by international audits including SEDEX 4P and ISO9001 certification.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Need a specific finish or unusual shape? Send the artwork and intended use for a production recommendation.

How do two-tone reversible (flip) sequin patches work?

Reversible sequin patches are crafted using double-sided sequins that are stitched on one edge. When brushed in one direction, side A shows one color or design; when brushed in the opposite direction, side B flips over to show a second color or design.

Can small text or intricate logos be made entirely with sequins?

Sequin discs (3mm or 5mm) are best suited for filling larger color areas. For complex logos with small typography or sharp outlines, we recommend a Sequin + Embroidery Hybrid Patch, where sequins fill the main body and fine embroidery thread stitches the crisp text and borders.

Are sequin patches washable, and how should they be cared for?

Yes! Our sequin patches are machine-stitched onto sturdy backing fabrics. For garments with sequin patches, we recommend turning the garment inside out before washing on a gentle cycle, or hand washing to preserve maximum brilliance and prevent snagging.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom sequin patches?

Our standard MOQ is 50 pieces per design. For multi-color or flip-sequin projects, please contact our team for custom wholesale volume discounts.

Are your sequin patches safe for children's apparel?

Absolutely. All our sequin materials, threads, and backing fabrics are non-toxic, lead-free, and fully certified under US CPSIA and EU EN71 textile safety standards. We ensure smooth, non-scratch border finishing specifically for kids' fashion lines.
