Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Need a specific finish or unusual shape? Send the artwork and intended use for a production recommendation.

Which yarns can be used for custom beanies?

Available options include acrylic, cotton, wool, merino wool, recycled polyester and blended yarns.

Can our logo be knitted directly into the beanie?

Yes. Jacquard knitting can integrate suitable logos and repeating patterns directly into the knitted structure.

Can we customize the beanie size and fit?

Yes. The opening circumference, crown height, cuff height and stretch can be adjusted for adult or youth sizing.

Are custom pom-poms available?

Yes. We can produce solid-color, multicolor, yarn and faux-fur pom-poms, including removable options.

Will you provide a sample for fit approval?

Yes. Sampling is recommended to confirm the dimensions, elasticity, colors, knit texture and logo position.
