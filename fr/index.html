Custom Metal Products

As a leading custom metal products manufacturer, we specialize in a wide range of high-quality metal gifts and promotional items. Our factory produces custom badges, medals, challenge coins, lapel pins, keychains, golf markers, divot tools, bottle openers, and metal accessories with precision and durability. We support a variety of decoration techniques including soft enamel, hard enamel, UV printing, laser engraving, and electroplating in gold, silver, nickel, and antique finishes. With more than 40 years of manufacturing experience, we can handle OEM projects, low-volume orders, and large-scale wholesale production with flexibility. From corporate awards and military medals to commemorative coins and event souvenirs, we deliver factory-direct pricing, skilled craftsmanship, and fast turnaround. Add your logo, custom artwork, or personal message to create memorable items. Ideal for brand promotion, events, donations, and collection series, our environmentally conscious metal gifts meet international quality standards.

Custom Patches & Labels

Raise your brand presence with custom patches and woven labels made with careful detail and premium quality. We manufacture embroidered patches, woven patches, chenille patches, PVC patches, leather patches, and garment labels, all customizable in logo, shape, size, and color. Using advanced embroidery equipment and eco-friendly materials, we create durable pieces with vivid, precise finishing. Ideal for fashion labels, military patches, sports team logos, and promotional giveaways, our OEM service and factory-direct pricing support both small runs and large-scale production. Perfect for apparel, hats, bags, uniforms, and accessories, with multiple backing options such as iron-on, sew-on, Velcro, and adhesive.

Custom Lanyards

We are a professional lanyard manufacturer offering custom lanyards for events, trade shows, schools, offices, and promotional campaigns. Materials include polyester, nylon, woven, sublimation, and eco-friendly RPET. In addition to screen printing, heat transfer, and jacquard weaving, we offer accessories such as metal hooks, card holders, safety buckles, and retractable reels. From small batches to mass production, we provide high-quality OEM supply with fast delivery.
