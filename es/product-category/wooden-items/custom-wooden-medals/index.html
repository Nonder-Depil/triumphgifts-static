Custom Wooden Medals

Custom wooden medals, an eco-friendly alternative to traditional metal awards. Perfect for sports events, school competitions, community races and corporate team-building activities, these medals combine sustainability with unique charm.

We craft medals from high-quality beech, maple, bamboo or walnut wood, offering laser engraving for intricate designs, UV printing for full-color graphics, and cut-out shapes for added creativity. Each medal can include custom ribbons, painted edges or natural finishes for a distinctive look.

Our factory allow custom shapes, sizes and wood types, as well as personalized packaging such as kraft boxes or fabric pouches, making wooden medals a meaningful and eco-conscious award choice.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Need a specific finish or unusual shape? Send the artwork and intended use for a production recommendation.

Can you produce wooden medals in any shape?

Yes. We can create round, square, shield-shaped and custom-cut wooden medals based on logos, mascots, landmarks or event artwork. Very thin or fragile sections may need adjustment before production.

Which wood is best for custom wooden medals?

Basswood is lightweight and suitable for detailed engraving, while beech provides greater strength. Bamboo works well for natural-themed events, and walnut offers a darker, more premium appearance.

Will every wooden medal have exactly the same color?

Natural grain and color may vary slightly between pieces. We control the material batch and surface finish to keep the overall order consistent while preserving the natural character of the wood.

Can each medal include a different name or race category?

Yes. Variable names, distances, categories, rankings or serial numbers can be laser engraved. Please provide the personalization data in a clearly organized file before production.

Can I order a sample before mass production?

Yes. We recommend a physical sample when the medal includes complex cut-outs, layered wood, special printing or a customized ribbon. Production begins after the sample or artwork is approved.

How are wooden medals packaged for international shipping?

Available packaging includes individual OPP bags, paper envelopes, backing cards and gift boxes. Medals and ribbons are arranged carefully to reduce scratching and tangling during transportation.
