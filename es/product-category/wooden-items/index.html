Wooden Items

We are an experienced manufacturer of custom wooden gifts, combining eco-friendly materials with exquisite craftsmanship. Our product range includes wooden keychains, magnets, lapel pins, medals, name tags, trophies, plaques, awards, bookmarks, and coasters, all customizable with laser engraving, UV printing, or screen printing.

Perfect for corporate events, eco-friendly campaigns, awards ceremonies, or souvenir shops, our wooden items offer a sustainable and premium look. We support OEM projects, unique shape customization, and factory-direct wholesale pricing.

Each product is made with sustainably sourced wood, ensuring a beautiful natural finish while promoting environmental responsibility.
