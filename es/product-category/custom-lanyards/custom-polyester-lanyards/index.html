Custom Polyester Lanyards

Our custom polyester lanyards are the most versatile and widely used option for corporate branding, ID card holders, trade shows, school events, and employee uniforms. Crafted from high-density, soft yet durable polyester webbing, they provide a comfortable wearing experience with excellent color retention.

You can choose silkscreen printing for bold, simple logos, or full-color sublimation for vibrant gradients and detailed graphics. We offer a wide range of attachments including swivel hooks, lobster clasps, oval clips, and safety breakaways for secure use. For additional functionality, you can add detachable buckles, badge holders, or even a built-in bottle opener clip.

As a factory specializing in OEM lanyard customization, we offer custom widths, lengths, Pantone color matching, and eco-friendly dyeing options. Whether you need low MOQ for a small event or bulk wholesale production for nationwide promotions, we ensure fast sampling, consistent quality, and competitive pricing.

Production process

How Your Custom Products Are Made

  1. 01Artwork & Proofing

    Submit your vector logo or design. Our design team provides a 1:1 digital layout proof within 24 hours for your review.

  2. 02Pre-Production Sample

    We produce a physical sample to confirm webbing texture, Pantone color matching, logo clarity, and attachment stitching.

  3. 03Webbing & Printing

    High-precision weaving, screen printing, or dye-sublimation heat transfer is executed on automated production lines.

  4. 04Cutting & Hardware Assembly

    Straps are ultrasonic-cut to exact lengths and securely assembled with your selected clips, buckles, and badge reels.

  5. 05100% Quality Inspection

    Every single lanyard undergoes rigorous visual, tensile, and stitching inspection to ensure flawless quality.

  6. 06Packaging & Fast Delivery

    Products are packaged in bundles or individual polybags and shipped via express courier directly to your door.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Need a specific finish or unusual shape? Send the artwork and intended use for a production recommendation.

What are custom polyester lanyards used for?

Custom polyester lanyards are widely used for company promotions, school ID badges, events, conferences, sports tournaments and volunteer identification.

Can you print full-color sublimated logos on polyester lanyards?

Yes. We offer single-sided and double-sided full-color dye-sublimation printing for your custom logo and artwork.

What width options do you supply for custom polyester lanyards?

Common widths include 15 mm narrow lanyards and standard 20 mm lanyards. Custom sizes and lengths are available.

Can I add safety breakaway, metal hooks or badge clips to my lanyards?

Absolutely. Multiple attachments including safety breakaways, metal hooks and plastic ID clips can be fitted.

Do you provide free artwork proof for custom polyester lanyard orders?

Yes, free digital design proof is offered before production approval.

What is your typical lead-time for bulk custom polyester lanyards?

Standard production lead-time is 7-10 working days after artwork confirmation. Physical pre-samples take 3-5 working days.
