Frequently Asked Questions

Here you’ll find answers to the most common questions about our custom promotional products, corporate gift manufacturing, and order process.

At Triumph Gifts, we’re proud to be a professional custom gift manufacturer with decades of experience producing high-quality items such as lapel pins, medals, badges, keychains, lanyards, embroidered patches, and more. Whether you're a first-time customer or a long-term partner, we’re here to make your experience smooth, efficient, and fully supported.

This FAQ section is designed to guide you through every step — from understanding materials and choosing the right product, to shipping, payment, and after-sales service. We know that every order is unique, and our team is always ready to answer questions, recommend solutions, and offer expert advice.

Can’t find what you're looking for?

We’re just one message away. Contact us anytime at sales@triumphgifts.com — our responsive, multilingual team will be happy to assist you within 24 hours.

Looking to get started? Request a Free Quote now!

1. What products can you customize?

We customize medals, lapel pins, challenge coins, keychains, belt buckles, patches, labels, lanyards, silicone items, PVC products, luggage tags, trophies, plaques, and promotional gift sets.

2. Can I use my own logo or artwork?

Yes. You can send your logo, sketch, reference image, or existing design file. Our team will review it and prepare production artwork before manufacturing.

3. What file format should I provide?

Vector files such as AI, EPS, PDF, or SVG are preferred. High-resolution PNG, JPG, or PSD files can also be used for initial quotation and artwork preparation.

4. What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on product type, size, material, and production process. Many custom metal and silicone items can start from small trial quantities.

5. Can you make samples before mass production?

Yes. We can arrange physical samples after artwork approval so you can confirm size, color, finish, and overall quality before bulk production.

6. How long does production take?

Lead time depends on product complexity and quantity. Simple samples usually take several working days, while bulk orders are scheduled after sample or artwork approval.

7. Can I choose plating colors and enamel colors?

Yes. Common plating options include gold, silver, nickel, black nickel, antique gold, antique silver, copper, brass, and more. Enamel colors can follow Pantone references.

8. Can you help improve my design?

Yes. Our team can suggest size, material, finishing, color, and attachment options to make your product easier to produce and more suitable for your budget.

9. Do you offer custom packaging?

Yes. Options include poly bags, velvet boxes, paper cards, blister packaging, printed gift boxes, and custom retail packaging.

10. Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide by express courier, air freight, sea freight, or other agreed shipping methods according to order requirements.

11. Which countries do you usually export to?

We work with customers in North America, Europe, Australia, the Middle East, Asia, and many other regions.

12. Can I get a quotation quickly?

Yes. Send product type, size, quantity, artwork, material, finish, packaging, and destination country for a faster and more accurate quotation.

13. Can you match a sample I already have?

Yes. Please send clear photos, dimensions, and any available physical sample information. We will evaluate the best production method.

14. Do you provide OEM and ODM service?

Yes. We support both OEM production based on customer artwork and ODM development for new product ideas.

15. Are your products suitable for corporate events?

Yes. Our products are widely used for brand promotion, company events, sports awards, school programs, military recognition, clubs, and retail merchandise.

16. How do you control quality?

We confirm artwork before production, inspect important production steps, check finished products, and package goods carefully before shipment.

17. Can I reorder the same design later?

Yes. We keep production records so repeat orders can be arranged more efficiently.

18. Can you handle urgent orders?

Urgent orders can be reviewed case by case. Please share your deadline, quantity, and delivery destination as early as possible.

19. How do I start an order?

Send us your idea, artwork, product type, quantity, and delivery requirements. Our sales team will confirm details, quotation, artwork, production schedule, and payment terms.

20. Can I split my order into multiple shipping addresses?

Yes. Multi-address shipping can be arranged when confirmed before shipment and may require additional packing or freight charges.
